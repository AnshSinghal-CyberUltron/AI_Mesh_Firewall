      statuses 200, 201, 201 (not recorded; the later DELETE of r3 returned 204), 204;
      Policy.version stays 1 after every one of them.

    A second PATCH with version 1 after that policy-field write: 409.
    -> The 409 check covers policy fields only. N-11; S6a/S6b; B13.

P8  Sibling events of one request (Review A, A-N8)                               CONFIRMED, with a nuance
