2026-09-24T05:47Z  Provider faults (fault 6) are applied to synthprov itself, never through an extra proxy hop (a
  proxy would add latency to every request of the run):
  - (a) kill = SIGKILL of synthprov, restarted as a new generation (fresh record dir) after 60 s. synthprov flushes
    records every 1 s, so records of requests that completed <= ~1 s before the SIGKILL are lost -> those requests are
    unjoinable and reported as such.
  - (b) 5xx = SIGTERM synthprov (listeners close at once, in-flight streams complete) + faultsrv (scripts/tools/faultsrv,
    Go, static) binds the same ports within ms and answers every request 500 + OpenAI envelope, recording x-request-id.
  - (c) hang = SIGSTOP 60 s then SIGCONT (TCP stays up; kernel accepts into the backlog; no bytes flow).
    Caveat for C4: requests accepted by the kernel while synthprov is stopped are only "received" by synthprov after
    SIGCONT; their pre-receive wait is charged to T_addon (client clock) -> C4 inside the hang window is provider time.
