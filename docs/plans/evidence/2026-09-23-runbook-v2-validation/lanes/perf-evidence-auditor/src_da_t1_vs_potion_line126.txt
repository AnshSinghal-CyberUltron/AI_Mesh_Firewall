## 0. THE SHORT VERSION

The synthesis's verdict — **NOT ACHIEVABLE as locked** — survives. I attacked it and could not move it. Two of its numbers are wrong (I corrected one by direct measurement, 8.60 ms → 1.67 ms), which moves the total from 126.0 ms to **≈ 119 ms**. A 9.9× miss instead of a 10.5× miss is not a different answer.

**But the synthesis answered the wrong question, and so did every reviewer except one.**

The whole pass asks: *can we run a semantic classifier inside 12 ms?* No. Fine. It then recommends shipping **Tier-1-only as the default posture** and certifying 12 ms for it — and **nobody in seven evidence agents and five adversarial reviewers measured what Tier-1-only actually detects.** I did:

| Measured this session, real `InputScanner._scan_prompt_sync` at HEAD | Result |
|---|---|
| Bare English prompt injections, same family as the shipped regexes | **3 / 10 detected** `[M]` |
| Bare English prompt injections, paraphrased with disjoint trigger vocabulary | **0 / 10 detected** `[M]` |
| Benign prompts containing any inline-backtick code | **5 / 5 HARD-BLOCKED**, `command_injection`, confidence 1.00 `[M]` |
| Ordinary agentic tool descriptions (my own list, not R5's) | **3 / 12 HARD-BLOCKED**, `data_leakage`, confidence 1.00 `[M]` |

The recommended shipping posture is **near-blind on the primary threat and hostile to ordinary developer traffic, simultaneously**. `` r"`[^`]+`" `` is a literal `command_injection` pattern at `scanner.py` (`ATTACK_PATTERNS["command_injection"]`), and `command_injection`/`data_leakage` are explicitly excluded from the explanatory-mention carve-out (`scanner.py:1197-1202`), so the block is terminal at tier_1 and never reaches Tier-2.

**That reframes the decision entirely.** This is not "12 ms vs 126 ms". It is: **the product's detection value lives almost entirely in the layer that costs 100× the SLO, and the layer that fits the SLO has never been measured and does not work.** A plan that ships posture (4) and publishes "12 ms p50" is publishing a latency number for a configuration whose security value is, on the only measurement anyone has taken, close to zero.

Everything else in this document is detail.

## 2. THE EXPERIMENT NOBODY RAN — and it decides the plan's recommendation

The synthesis's option (1) — its **funded research track** — is `potion-base-8M`. R2 attacked it with raw cosine geometry and declared it fatally refuted. R5 attacked it with an order-invariance check. **Neither trained a head.** A head is exactly what the design proposes, so neither result was decisive.

I trained it. `scratchpad/da/potion_probe.py`, `model2vec` 0.9.0, the checkpoint already on disk, benign corpus = 72 real varied English documents harvested from this repo, injections inserted **replacing** an equal number of words (length held constant), **documents disjoint** between train and test, **injection phrasings disjoint** between train and test, L2-regularised logistic head, ROC-AUC + recall at fixed FPR.

Two test families: **TEST-A** = unseen wording that still shares trigger vocabulary; **TEST-B** = paraphrases with *disjoint* trigger vocabulary (no ignore/disregard/instruction/prompt/system/override/forget/reveal) — the only family that tests whether the embedding adds anything over the keyword matcher it would replace.

| window size | ≈ tokens | AUC (A, same family) | AUC (B, disjoint vocab) | recall @1 % FPR (B) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 words | ~21 | **0.9995** | **0.9498** | **0.377** |
| 48 words | ~62 | 0.9566 | 0.7708 | 0.060 |
| **200 words** | **~260 — the deployed window** | **0.7397** | **0.6017** | **0.028** |

`[M]` all six cells. Raw cosine benign-vs-poisoned at 200 words: median 0.9937. Order-invariance sanity check: `cos(original, shuffled) = 1.000000` — R2's observation replicates exactly.

**Adjudication, three ways:**

1. **R2's verdict is CONFIRMED at the deployed configuration.** At a 256-token window a trained head reaches AUC 0.60 and **2.8 % recall at 1 % FPR** on true paraphrases. That is not a security control. Lever A, **as specified in the synthesis**, is dead — now on the right evidence rather than on a suggestive geometry argument.
2. **R2's reasoning is OVERSTATED.** "No trained head can recover it" is measurably false: at 21-token windows the same head reaches AUC 0.95 / 37.7 % recall @1 % FPR on paraphrases. The failure mechanism is **dilution**, not order-invariance. That distinction matters because dilution has a lever (window size) and order-invariance does not.
3. **The lever does not survive the arithmetic.** Small windows mean many windows, and **FPR compounds**: 51 windows at 1 % per-window FPR gives a **40 % per-request** false-positive rate. Holding a 1 % per-request budget across 51 windows requires ~0.02 % per-window FPR, far below anything I can even estimate with 600 negatives — and recall there is well under the 2.8 % floor already measured at 0.1 %. **Cost was never the constraint** (my §1.6 F3 measurement: potion at the band is ~1.5–2 ms total). **The multiple-comparisons FPR budget is the constraint, and no document in this pass models it.**

**The finding the whole pass missed, and the reason this matters more than any of the above:**

I then asked the question the plan never asks — *what does the layer we are keeping actually catch?* — and ran the **same two families** through the real `InputScanner._scan_prompt_sync` at HEAD:

```
SAME-FAMILY (shares trigger words)      Tier-1 detects  3/10
DISJOINT-VOCAB PARAPHRASE               Tier-1 detects  0/10
```

`[M]`, `scratchpad/da/t1_vs_potion.py`. And on the false-positive side, `scratchpad/da/t1_fp.py`:

```
"How do I use the `subprocess.run` helper in Python?"   -> block / command_injection / 1.00
"Explain what `SELECT * FROM users` does in SQL."       -> block / command_injection / 1.00
"What does the `--dry-run` flag do?"                    -> block / command_injection / 1.00
"Please review this snippet: ```python print('hi')```"  -> block / command_injection / 1.00
"The config key is `enforcement_mode` and I want ..."   -> block / command_injection / 1.00
                                                           5/5 benign prompts hard-blocked
```

Root cause, verified at source: `ATTACK_PATTERNS["command_injection"]` contains the literal pattern `` r"`[^`]+`" `` — *any* pair of backticks with content between them. `command_injection` and `data_leakage` are excluded from the explanatory-mention carve-out (`scanner.py:1197-1202`), so the verdict is terminal, `tier_1`, confidence 1.00, and never reaches Tier-2.

**So:** the posture the synthesis recommends shipping as the default, and certifying 12 ms for, detects 0/10 paraphrased injections and blocks 5/5 benign developer questions. **Three of the four options on the table (1, 2 and 4) rest on that posture being a viable product.** On the only measurement anyone has taken, it is not.

## 4. TOP RISKS, RANKED — what would actually kill this in production

1. **The default posture does not detect the threat and blocks ordinary traffic.** `[M]` 0/10 paraphrased injections detected; 5/5 benign backtick prompts and 3/12 ordinary tool descriptions hard-blocked at confidence 1.00. This is not a latency risk; it is an existence risk for the product story, and it is measured, today, at HEAD. Everything below is secondary.
2. **Every gate in the plan is scored by an instrument that reports the firewall as free on streams.** R4 A1/A2/A3/A6 — confirmed at source. Ship the plan and Phase 1.1's "stop and revise" gate cannot fire, the 12 ms gate passes unconditionally, and the team will believe it succeeded. **Nothing else should be measured until this is fixed.**
3. **A partial cold start silently disables the operator policy layer for a subset of tenants, forever, while `/health` reports 200.** R2 H-2 / R3 A2, confirmed at source. Live today. 16 workers × 6 nodes = 96 independent SCANs, each able to stop at a different point, so the same tenant is enforced on some workers and not others with no way to tell which served a given request.
4. **An un-synced tenant silently inherits the platform-default org's firewall posture.** R3 A3 — confirmed **reachable** (`models.py:1368-1370` writes `firewall:config:default`). Direction undetermined, so treat as fail-open. The in-repo fix already exists (`get_own_config`) and was applied to the MCP path but not to the chat hot path.
5. **No admission control anywhere, and the one operator cap is silently inert.** R2 H-3 — `uvicorn.workers.CONFIG_KWARGS` verified `[M]`. Rate limits bound rate, not concurrency. Raising `timeoutSec` to 3600 without first adding `limit_concurrency` converts a routine upstream stall into a metastable failure.
6. **Fail-closed kill switch × a dependency with tens-of-seconds unplanned failover = a designed-in, unsized, all-tenant outage,** which one tenant's connection-pool exhaustion can trigger for everyone. R2 H-5 + S-4.
7. **Config/policy staleness is unbounded after any pub/sub disconnect** while L8 signs 50 ms for the smallest key family. R3 A4.
8. **The output-path `scan_degraded` floor is dead code** (S-1) and input/output share one breaker key (S-2) which counts globally across tenants (S-3). Confirmed by three reviewers independently.
9. **p99 under concurrency is unestablished for every posture.** All 17 budget lines are p50, C=1, idle-box, x86, loopback. L6's 40 ms p99 clause has no evidence behind it.
10. **Nothing was measured on `c4a-standard-16`.** Every millisecond is Intel x86. The Arm transfer factor is `[D]`, including the 62 % FLOP-efficiency term the whole capacity argument rests on.
11. **Language × region mismatch:** admission in characters, SLO in tokens, deployment locked to asia-south1, `len//4` token estimator under-charging Devanagari 4.1×. R5 F5.
12. **Telemetry discards enforcement evidence during exactly the failure the plan expects.** R2 H-7.

## 6. THE HONESTY LIST — what we must NOT claim

**Latency**
1. **Do not claim "12 ms" without a posture, a prompt band, and a percentile.** Today the honest sentence is: *"≈ 119 ms p50 with semantic classification on; a Tier-1-only posture is projected at 3–5 ms p50 once rewritten, with p99 unmeasured."*
2. **Do not publish any streaming latency number produced by the current bench.** On streams it measures the customer's model, not us (R4 A1, confirmed at source). Any streaming figure quoted today is provider TTFT wearing our name.
3. **Do not claim a p99.** No p99 evidence exists for any posture under realistic concurrency. L6's 40 ms clause is unbacked.
4. **Do not claim any figure "on Axion / c4a-standard-16".** Nothing was measured there.
5. **Do not claim N ≈ 85 RPS/host.** It is a 32–64-token number; at the L5 band today's host does ~5 RPS.
6. **Do not claim 100 k RPS.** 118 nodes = $43,290/mo of compute alone, 8.7× the envelope.

**Detection — the part that has never been said out loud**

7. **We cannot claim the default posture detects prompt injection.** Measured `[M]`: 3/10 same-family, **0/10 paraphrased**. If Tier-1-only ships as the default, the product's default configuration does not do the thing the product is named after.
8. **We cannot claim a low false-positive rate.** Measured `[M]`: **5/5** benign inline-code prompts and **3/12** ordinary tool descriptions hard-blocked at confidence 1.00, terminally. A developer-facing customer will hit this in the first session.
9. **We cannot claim tool/function-calling support at the 1024-token band.** ~6–7 ordinary tools tip the request into `block/dos/context_length_exceeded`.
10. **We cannot claim a supported prompt length above ~1,970 tokens** (English prose; ~800 in Hindi, ~750 in Tamil — in an India-only deployment).
11. **We cannot claim streaming "block" is a block.** It is a truncation: measured 260 of 800 characters delivered, then an error frame. Overlapping widens the released prefix. Either withhold fully or document truncation semantics — but do not let an operator who selected "block" believe they got one.
12. **We cannot claim output responses are Tier-2 scanned end-to-end today.** `scan_degraded` is dead code on the output path (S-1, confirmed by three reviewers), so on a Bedrock outage or breaker-OPEN streams are delivered Tier-1-only, fail-open, with no operator signal — while the telemetry attests otherwise. **This is a false attestation in a compliance record and it is live.**
13. **We cannot claim per-tenant policy isolation.** An un-synced tenant inherits the platform-default org's posture (R3 A3, confirmed reachable).
14. **We cannot claim policy enforcement is fail-closed.** A partial cold start leaves a subset of tenants unenforced with `/health` green and no reconciliation, ever.
15. **We cannot claim a bounded revocation or config-propagation time.** `auth:epoch` does not exist; config/policy staleness after a pub/sub disconnect is unbounded.
16. **We cannot claim complete enforcement records.** Telemetry drops the whole batch on a Redis error and the oldest events on overflow, with no retry, DLQ or spill — precisely during the failures we expect.
17. **We cannot claim Tier-2 sees the whole response.** `_head_tail` at `bedrock_scanner.py:63-72` silently drops the middle of any answer over 10,000 characters.
18. **We cannot claim availability during cache maintenance.** Under L8 as signed, every Memorystore failover — including scheduled maintenance — is a full-product 503.

**Process**

19. **Do not present target values as measurements.** The "2.39 ms `[M]` component-wise" row is a sum of unbuilt targets, two of which the same document calls unreachable. It has already misled one reader in this very pass.
20. **Do not claim `potion-base-8M` is a viable Tier-2 replacement.** Measured `[M]`: AUC 0.60 / 2.8 % recall @1 % FPR at the deployed window. It may be worth research at ~20-token windows, where the unsolved problem is the per-request FPR budget, not the FLOPs.
21. **Do not claim the off-host option was evaluated.** It was rejected on a price from a different cloud.
