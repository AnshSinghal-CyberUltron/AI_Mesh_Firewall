     9	Vault check errors are classified into two kinds:
    10	
    11	* **auth/config errors** (HTTP 401/403, invalid API keys, bad DB credentials,
    12	  permission denied) — these are persistent misconfigurations. They are logged
    13	  at ERROR once per cooldown window (not per request) and counted in the
    14	  ``amf_gateway_embedding_vault_errors_total`` Prometheus counter
    15	  (kind="auth") on the gateway /metrics surface.
    16	* **transient errors** (timeouts, 5xx, connection resets) — logged at WARNING

    18	
    19	Environment variables
    20	---------------------
